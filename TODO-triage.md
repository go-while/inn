## Where to start

- The TODO entries that can go at once (done or obsolete): T005, T033, T040, T044, T047, T056, T075, T083, T106.
- Three bugs to report separately:
  - T055: rnews can delete a spooled batch when the decompression child fails. Confirmed by a test run at 761db103b: a gzip batch cut short delivered 8 of 20 articles, and rnews removed the spooled file.
  - T042: innd restarts a channel or exploder feed at once when its process exits, without the chanretrytime pause (found by reading the code).
  - T041: a miscount in the innfeed statistics. A code comment says innfeed can count an article twice when it switches to no-CHECK mode, and accepts that (innfeed/connection.c:3918-3927); the TODO says the statistics do not add up.
- Four other entries worth considering first:
  - T013: ctlinnd newgroup and rmgroup reload newsfeeds and flush every feed.
  - T064: with remembertrash, a mangled copy from one peer makes innd refuse the good copy from the others.
  - T073: ctlinnd reload incoming.conf answers OK even when hosts could not be loaded.
  - T045: nntpget silently cuts article lines longer than 511 bytes (backends/nntpget.c:76, 104-105), so it changes the articles it fetches; moot if T089 retires nntpget.
